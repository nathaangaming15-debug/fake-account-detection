What the model reads

  • Follower / following balance Fabricated accounts typically follow far more people than follow them back — a lopsided ratio is one of the strongest tells.
  • Username & name composition Bulk-generated accounts often carry usernames padded with digits, and a full name that's identical to the username.
  • Profile completeness A picture, a bio, an external link, a public setting — genuine accounts tend to fill these in; fabricated ones rarely bother.
  • Posting behaviour Posting volume relative to audience size reveals accounts built for following, not for sharing.
